EIGHTEEN PONDS, EIGHTEEN ANSWERS

Each card is the winner of its own genetic algorithm: 36 creatures, 48 generations, starting from one cell. Each pond rewards a different way of living, and every pond also rewards looking good. The body is grown from genes; the six bars (speed, agility, reach, senses, armour, attack) are measured from the body, not assigned.

FOUR PONDS, GENERATION BY GENERATION

The same first cell in four ponds. Each card is the best creature alive at that generation.
