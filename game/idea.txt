USER'S WORDS
Simple, fun game simulating a genetic algorithm. Cute, funny, calm and beautiful. The user should get a sense of generations and the genetic-algorithm steps: random population; choose the best, breed and mutate; again.
The fitness function must be real and get better through the GA. Creatures evolve. Start from an amoeba; real genetic evolution; 100% unpredictable, different every play, completely new creatures and worlds. No goal. The player is nature. Two things uncapped: what you add to the pond, and what each generation grows/mutates. The GA must be very clear and present in the game. Server AI may be used later for small things (site owner); build locally first, fully working.

DECISIONS (Claude)
- Title: Primordia. 2D top-down glowing microscope pond.
- One season = one generation (~35s, fast-forward): Spring reproduce+crossover+mutate, Summer test (life), Autumn score fitness (energy bars), Winter select (low fitness fade).
- Permanent Evolution Panel: step bar, generation, population, avg-fitness graph, diversity, mutation log. Tap creature -> genome strip, parents, highlighted mutations, fitness. Family tree.
- Genome: open-ended recipe of parts + tiny neural-net brain + chemistry genes. Mutations add/remove parts, rewire brain. Every part costs energy. Capabilities discovered log ("First eye!").
- Start: soup of identical membrane blobs that eat and split.
- Player = nature, no goal/score: add anything by typing a word (properties: nutrition, heat, poison, light, stickiness...), weather/disasters, time control, observation.
- Field guide with procedural species names/descriptions; fossils; extinction = story.
- AI: single ask(task,input,schema) interface; offline backend (word table + hash fallback + procedural mutations) now; later server backend returning JSON + sanitized SVG, engine clamps and charges costs.
- Save with Plaxzy.save: seed, field guide, fossils.
